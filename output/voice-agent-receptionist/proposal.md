# Proposal: A friendly AI receptionist for Willow & Fern Hair Studio

Hi there,

## Missed calls while your hands are in the dye

With five of you fully booked and mid-colour or mid-cut most of the day, nobody can get to the phone. You counted dozens of missed calls last month, and most people don't leave a voicemail. They ring the next salon down the road instead.

The calls you do answer are often the same questions: Sunday opening, the price of a balayage, parking, moving an appointment.

## How we would solve it

We'd set up an AI voice receptionist (a computer voice that holds a natural conversation) that picks up when you can't. It would:

1. **Sound warm and chatty,** set up to match how you talk to your regulars, not a stiff phone menu.
2. **Answer the common questions** using answers you give us.
3. **Book, move and cancel appointments** through your online booking system. You weren't sure it connects, so we'll check first. If it doesn't, the receptionist takes the request and passes it to you.
4. **Know about patch tests,** so colour bookings are flagged for one first.
5. **Handle prices carefully.** As they vary by stylist, it only gives answers you approve.
6. **Take a message and tell you** whenever it can't help.

Your landline number stays the same. Whether unanswered calls can be passed to the receptionist depends on your phone provider, so we'll check that too.

You'd like to hear it before you commit, which makes complete sense. We'll talk through options for a sample on the discovery call.

## How we would work together

- A relaxed discovery call, with no tech talk, to learn how your salon runs.
- We build in small steps and you try each one before we move on.
- A simple walkthrough at the end for the whole team.

## A few questions before we start

1. Which online booking system do you use?
2. Which company provides your landline?
3. When the receptionist takes a message, how would you like to hear about it (for example a text or an email), and who should get it?
4. As prices vary by stylist, should it give a starting price, a price per stylist, or say the salon will confirm?
5. Which services need a patch test, and how do you keep track of who has had one?
