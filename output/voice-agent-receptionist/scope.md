# Scope: A friendly AI receptionist for Willow & Fern Hair Studio

What we will build, what is not included and what we need from you.

## Project summary

With everyone mid-colour or mid-cut, the salon misses dozens of calls, loses new clients and answers the same questions repeatedly. We will set up a friendly AI voice receptionist (a computer voice that holds a natural conversation) that answers when you can't.

## What is included

- Answering calls you miss, on your existing landline number (to be confirmed, see Open questions 2).
- A warm, chatty voice matched to your style, approved by you before going live.
- Answers to common questions (opening times, parking), in your wording.
- Booking, moving and cancelling through your booking system (to be confirmed, see Open questions 1). If it can't connect, requests are passed to you.
- Colour bookings flagged for a patch test, for the services you list (see Open questions 5).
- Price answers only as you approve, since prices vary by stylist (see Open questions 4).
- A message taken whenever it can't help (to be confirmed, see Open questions 3).
- A simple walkthrough for the team at hand-over.

## What is not included

- Changing your phone number or provider.
- The cost of calls, the AI voice service, your phone line or booking system.
- Outgoing calls (for example reminder calls).
- Allergy or skin reaction advice (it only flags patch tests).
- Changes to your booking system's own setup, such as adding services or stylists.
- Ongoing support or changes after hand-over.

Anything not listed above can be discussed and added separately.

## What the client must provide

- Staff or admin access to your booking system, shared securely (never passwords by email or in documents).
- Your phone provider's details and permission to pass unanswered calls on.
- Your answers to the common questions, in your own words.
- Your services, which need a patch test, and how to handle prices.
- Who should receive messages, and how.
- One person (likely you) to answer questions and try each stage.

## Build steps

1. **Discovery call:** a relaxed chat, including options for hearing a sample voice before you commit.
2. **Voice and answers:** you listen and suggest changes.
3. **Booking link:** you try test bookings (or the message fallback).
4. **Patch tests and prices:** you check them with test calls.
5. **Messages:** you check a test message arrives.
6. **Phone link:** you make a test call to your landline.
7. **Review round:** the team sends one combined list of fixes. We fix anything that does not work as described here.
8. **Hand-over:** a simple walkthrough for the team.

## Open questions

1. Which online booking system do you use?
2. Which company provides your landline?
3. When the receptionist takes a message, how would you like to hear about it (for example a text or an email), and who should get it?
4. As prices vary by stylist, should it give a starting price, a price per stylist, or say the salon will confirm?
5. Which services need a patch test, and how do you keep track of who has had one?

Pricing will be confirmed after the discovery call, once these are answered.
