# Scope: A simple Airtable CRM for Northpaw Studio

This sets out what we will build, what is not included and what we need from you, so we all agree before work starts.

## Project summary

Northpaw's client info sits in a messy Google Sheet, deals live in someone's head, and two proposals went cold last quarter because nobody chased them. We will build a lean Airtable CRM that the whole team of four can use day to day, with reminders so follow-ups stop slipping.

## What is included

- A **Clients** table with contacts, company and a status field (status options agreed on the discovery call).
- A **Deals** table with stage, value, expected close and owner, linked to each client.
- A pipeline board view, so deals can be dragged from stage to stage.
- A **Follow-ups** table for tasks, each linked to a client or deal, with a due date and an owner.
- Reminders when a follow-up is due. How they are delivered (email or a chat tool) depends on your setup and Airtable plan, to be confirmed (see Open questions 2, 3 and 5).
- A founders' dashboard showing open deals and overdue follow-ups. (A total pipeline value could be discussed on the discovery call as a possible addition, but it is not part of this scope.)
- Moving your Google Sheet (around 150 rows) into Airtable, merging duplicates and tidying formats, with a list of any rows we could not sort out confidently for you to decide on.
- A last contact date on each client, pulled from your email, only if your email platform and Airtable plan allow that connection. We will confirm this before building it (see Open questions 1 and 3).
- A short walkthrough for the whole team at hand-over.

## What is not included

- Ongoing support or changes after the hand-over walkthrough.
- The cost of your Airtable plan or any plan upgrade.
- Importing data from anywhere other than the Google Sheet.
- Invoicing, project management or retainer tracking.
- Sending marketing or bulk emails from Airtable.
- Correcting details we cannot check (for example an out-of-date phone number). These go on the flagged list instead.

Anything not listed above can be discussed and added separately.

## What the client must provide

- An invite to your Airtable workspace as a collaborator (no passwords shared in documents or emails).
- A copy of the Google Sheet.
- Your pipeline stages and the status options you use for clients, even if they are informal today.
- The names and roles of everyone who will use the CRM.
- Access to your email and chat tool for reminders and the last contact date, once we confirm which ones.
- One person to answer questions, review each stage and decide on flagged rows.

## Build steps

1. **Discovery call:** agree on fields, stages, statuses and tools.
2. **Base structure:** set up the tables and links. You check the fields.
3. **Data move:** import and tidy the Google Sheet. You review a sample and the flagged list.
4. **Pipeline board:** build the board view. Any extra filtered views (for example each person's own follow-ups) are agreed on the discovery call, not added by default. You try it out.
5. **Reminders and email link:** set up reminders through the confirmed channel and, if confirmed as possible, the last contact date. You test a reminder.
6. **Dashboard:** the founders check it shows what they need.
7. **Review round:** the team tries the CRM on real work and sends one combined list of fixes. We fix anything that does not work as described in this scope. New features and later changes are not part of this round.
8. **Hand-over:** a short walkthrough for the team.

## Open questions

1. Which email platform does the team use? This decides whether we can show the last contact date, and how.
2. Should reminders go to email or a chat tool? If chat, which one?
3. Which Airtable plan are you on? It affects which automations and dashboard features we can use.
4. What pipeline stages do you use today, even informally?
5. Who should receive reminders: each deal owner, or the founders as well?

Pricing will be confirmed after the discovery call, once these are answered.
