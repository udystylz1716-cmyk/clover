# Scope: Automatic lead capture and routing for Brightstone Home Renovations

What we will build, what is not included and what we need from you.

## Project summary

Leads from your three forms are copied by hand from a shared inbox into your CRM, so some are missed or doubled and callbacks are slow. We will build an n8n workflow (automatic steps) that captures, tidies, de-duplicates and routes every lead to the right rep.

## What is included

- Capture of the main contact and "get a quote" forms.
- Capture of the loft conversion form (to be confirmed, see Open questions 2).
- Tidying: names cleaned, postcodes formatted, project type tagged.
- A basic postcode check, plus paid enrichment if chosen (to be confirmed, see Open questions 5).
- Missing postcodes or project types flagged, not guessed.
- A duplicate check (for example by email and phone).
- Leads sent into your CRM with their source form (to be confirmed, see Open questions 1).
- Assignment to the right regional rep (see Open questions 3).
- An instant alert to that rep (to be confirmed, see Open questions 4).
- Testing every stage with made-up leads.
- A walkthrough for your office manager at hand-over.

## What is not included

- The weekly summary of leads by source (can be added separately).
- Changes to your website, forms or ad campaigns, beyond connecting forms.
- Costs of enrichment services, CRM or n8n plans.
- Cleaning up existing leads in your CRM or inbox.
- Changes to your n8n review alert.
- Ongoing support or changes after hand-over.
- Correcting unverifiable details, such as a mistyped phone number.

Anything not listed above can be discussed and added separately.

## What the client must provide

- Access to your n8n (see Open questions 6).
- CRM API access (a key that lets n8n add leads), shared securely, never by email.
- Access to each form or your website manager.
- Your postcode-to-region list and the rep for each region.
- Your alert channel and enrichment choices.
- Your office manager as main contact, checking each stage.

## Build steps

1. **Discovery call:** agree fields, region rules, alerts and enrichment.
2. **Form capture:** you check test leads arrive.
3. **Tidy and check:** you review sample test leads.
4. **Duplicate check:** you check test duplicates are caught.
5. **CRM and routing:** you check test leads in the CRM.
6. **Rep alerts:** a rep confirms a test alert arrived.
7. **Review round:** a final test run, fixing anything not working as described here.
8. **Hand-over:** a walkthrough, then real leads switch on.
