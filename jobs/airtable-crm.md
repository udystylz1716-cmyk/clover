# Airtable CRM build for a small creative agency

**Client:** Northpaw Studio (made-up)
**Industry:** Branding and content agency
**Company size:** 4 people (2 founders, 1 designer, 1 account manager)
**Budget:** $800 to $1,200

## Background

Branding and social content agency. Mostly retainer clients plus one-off brand projects. Growing, and our current setup isn't keeping up.

## Problem

Client info lives in a messy Google Sheet, deals live in someone's head, and follow-ups get forgotten. Two proposals went cold last quarter because nobody chased them. Not acceptable.

## What we need

Airtable CRM with:
- Clients (contacts, company, status)
- Deals / pipeline (stage, value, expected close, owner)
- Follow-ups / tasks with reminders
- Simple dashboard view for the founders

Should be easy for the whole team to use. We don't want something bloated.

## Details

- Want to migrate existing data from the Google Sheet (around 150 rows, some messy).
- Reminders ideally come through email or whatever we already use for chat.
- Would like it linked to our email somehow so we can see the last contact date.
- Already on an Airtable plan, not sure which one.
- Need a short handover / walkthrough at the end.

Send examples of Airtable bases you've built. Short proposals preferred.
