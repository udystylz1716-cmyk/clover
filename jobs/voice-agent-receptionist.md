# AI receptionist to answer our phone and book appointments (small salon!)

**Client:** Willow & Fern Hair Studio (made-up)
**Industry:** Hair and beauty salon
**Company size:** owner plus 4 stylists, one location
**Budget:** budget open, but we're a small business so please be sensible

## Background

Hi! I own a small, busy hair salon. There are five of us and we're fully booked most weeks, which is lovely, but it means nobody can answer the phone when we're mid-colour or mid-cut. Our hands are literally covered in dye half the day.

## The problem

We miss a LOT of calls. I checked last month and it was dozens. Some people leave a voicemail, most don't. I know we're losing new clients because they just ring the next salon down the road.

We also get the same questions over and over: "Are you open Sunday?", "How much is a balayage?", "Do you have parking?", "Can I move my appointment?". It takes up so much time.

## What we want

- An AI voice receptionist that answers the phone when we can't.
- It should sound friendly and natural, not like a robot. Our clients are mostly regulars and we're very chatty and personal.
- It should be able to book, move and cancel appointments.
- Answer the common questions.
- If it can't help, take a message and let us know somehow.

## Other details

- We use an online booking system already, clients can book through it on their phones. Not sure if it connects to things like this.
- We have a normal landline number that's been the same for years and I really don't want to change it.
- Some services need a patch test 48 hours before (colours mostly), so it needs to know that.
- Prices vary by stylist, which makes things a bit complicated.
- I'm not very techy, so please explain things simply!
- Would love to hear what it would actually sound like before we commit.
