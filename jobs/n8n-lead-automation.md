# n8n expert needed: capture, enrich and route website leads into our CRM

**Client:** Brightstone Home Renovations (made-up)
**Industry:** Home renovation and extensions
**Company size:** around 40 staff, 3 regional offices
**Budget:** roughly $1,500 to $2,500 fixed, open to discussion for the right person

## Background

We're a family-run renovation company that has grown quickly over the last few years. We do kitchens, bathrooms, loft conversions and full extensions. Most of our new work now comes through our website, plus a couple of landing pages we run for ad campaigns.

## The problem

Leads come in through three different forms (main contact form, a "get a quote" form and a landing page form for loft conversions). Right now they all land in a shared inbox. Someone in the office copies them into our CRM by hand, usually the next morning, sometimes later when it's busy.

This means:
- Some leads get missed or entered twice.
- We don't know which form or campaign a lead came from.
- Sales reps call people back slowly and we think we're losing jobs because of it.
- Half the leads have missing info (no postcode, no project type) so reps have to chase.

## What we want

- Use **n8n** to capture every form submission automatically.
- Enrich the lead where possible (company info if it's a business, maybe check the address or postcode, tag the project type).
- Send the clean lead into our CRM, assigned to the right regional sales rep.
- Notify the rep straight away.
- Something to stop duplicates.

We already use n8n for one small thing (a Slack-style alert for new reviews) so we're happy to stay on it. Not sure if we're on the cloud version or self-hosted, our previous developer set it up.

## Nice to have

- A simple weekly summary of leads by source.
- Ideally the reps can see where the lead came from inside the CRM.

## Other details

- Our CRM is one of the popular ones, it has an API.
- Lead volume varies a lot, quieter in winter, very busy in spring.
- Our office manager will be the main contact. She's not technical but knows the process inside out.
- Please tell us about similar n8n projects you've done.
